# The Definitive Five Nights at Freddy's Chronological Timeline
This document outlines the complete, highly detailed narrative timeline of the *Five Nights at Freddy's* franchise, spanning from the inception of Fazbear Entertainment in the late 1970s up to the events of *Five Nights at Freddy's: Into the Pit*. 

---

## ERA 1: The Genesis of Fazbear Entertainment (Late 1970s – 1983)

### 1. The Partnership of Henry and William
In the late 1970s, mechanical genius **Henry Emily** partners with charismatic businessman **William Afton**. Together, they establish **Fredbear's Family Diner** in New Harmony, Indiana. 
* **Henry's Role:** The creative force. He invents the revolutionary "Springlock Animatronics"—costumes that use a system of mechanical gears and locks, allowing them to be worn by performers or operate autonomously as robotic performers.
* **William's Role:** The business administrator, investor, and financial operator. Behind his professional exterior lies an escalating, deeply pathological envy of Henry's creative talent and a growing fixation on death, legacy, and human emotion.

### 2. The Springlock Technology
Henry designs two flagship characters: **Fredbear** (a golden bear) and **Spring Bonnie** (a golden rabbit).
> [!warning] The Danger of Springlocks
> The springlock mechanisms are notoriously unstable. If moisture, sudden movement, or a mechanical flaw triggers the locks while a human is inside, the packed robotic parts snap violently forward into their default positions, instantly crushing, impaling, and shredding the occupant.

### 3. The First Murder: The Death of Charlotte Emily (1983)
As business expands, the duo opens the first iteration of **Freddy Fazbear's Pizza**. Henry creates a security animatronic called the **Security Puppet** specifically to protect his young daughter, **Charlotte (Charlie) Emily**.
* **The Incident:** On a rainy night, a group of children lock Charlie outside the establishment. William Afton drives up, spots the isolated and crying girl, and in a fit of jealous rage against Henry, murders Charlie in the alleyway before driving off.
* **The First Possession:** The Security Puppet, attempting to fulfill its programming, goes out into the pouring rain to save Charlie. The water short-circuits its systems, causing it to collapse over Charlie's corpse in the alley. Charlie's soul binds with the Puppet through her intense agony and need for protection, becoming the first possessed animatronic.

### 4. The Bite of '83 (1983)
Back at Fredbear's Family Diner, William Afton's youngest son (commonly referred to as the **Crying Child** or **Evan Afton**) suffers severe psychological trauma. He is terrified of the animatronics, a fear exacerbated by his older brother, **Michael Afton**, who relentlessly bullies him using a Foxy mask.
* **The Prank gone Wrong:** On the Crying Child's birthday, Michael and his friends carry the screaming boy to the stage and force his head into the mouth of the performing animatronic, Fredbear.
* **The Bite:** The moisture from the child's tears triggers the springlocks in Fredbear's jaw. The mouth snaps shut with crushing force, crushing the child's skull.
* **The Aftermath:** The Crying Child is rushed to the hospital, slipping into a coma. Beside his hospital bed, Michael expresses profound regret. A voice (implied to be William or a manifestation of his grief/plushie) promises the boy: *"You're broken. I will put you back together."* The Crying Child dies shortly after, closing down Fredbear's Family Diner permanently.

## ERA 2: The Tragedy of 1985 & The Birth of Remnant

### 1. The Missing Children's Incident (MCI) — 1985
Following the closure of Fredbear's, focus shifts entirely to the corporate flagship: **Freddy Fazbear's Pizza** (the original 1985 location). William Afton's psychological descent accelerates after losing his youngest son. He becomes obsessed with the concept of immortality and the supernatural energy generated by extreme human suffering, later classified as **Agony** and **Remnant**.

* **The Date:** June 26, 1985.
* **The Method:** William dons the Spring Bonnie suit to gain the trust of young children visiting the pizzeria. He lures five children—**Susie, Jeremy, Gabriel, Fritz, and Cassidy**—into the safe room (a hidden room omitted from the animatronics' digital maps).
* **The Slaughter:** William murders all five children. He hides their corpses inside the operational animatronics: **Chica (Susie), Bonnie (Jeremy), Freddy (Gabriel), Foxy (Fritz)**, and the spare springlock suit, **Golden Freddy (Cassidy)**.
* **The Awakening:** The Puppet (Charlie), driven by an instinct to comfort the dead, guides the spirits of the murdered children, effectively "giving them life" by binding their souls to the metal hulls of the animatronics. 
* **The Closure:** Customers soon complain of a foul odor, blood, and mucus leaking from the eyes and mouths of the animatronics. The police arrest William Afton, but due to a lack of physical evidence (the bodies are never found), he is ultimately released. The restaurant is abandoned and boarded up.

> [!info] The "Into the Pit" Connection
> The events of 1985 serve as the bedrock for *Into the Pit*. The twisted memory of this exact multi-child slaughter becomes trapped as pure **Agony** within the ballpit of Jeff's Pizza (the repurposed 1985 building), manifesting decades later as a monstrous, organic iteration of the Yellow Rabbit.

## ERA 3: Circus Baby and the Afton Family Collapse (Mid-to-Late 1980s)

### 1. Circus Baby's Pizza World
Eager to conduct unchecked experiments on soul extraction and capture metrics, William Afton forms a shell company, **Afton Robotics LLC**, and designs a new line of advanced robots: the **Funtime Animatronics** (Circus Baby, Funtime Freddy, Funtime Foxy, Ballora).
* **The Hidden Purpose:** These animatronics are secretly equipped with internal storage tanks, claw mechanisms, and tracking systems engineered explicitly to capture and kill children in isolation.
* **The Death of Elizabeth Afton:** William warns his daughter, **Elizabeth**, to stay away from **Circus Baby**. Mesmerized by the robot, Elizabeth approaches Baby when she is alone. Circus Baby's automated ice cream dispenser lures her close, a mechanical claw extends from Baby's torso, pulls Elizabeth inside her internal structure, and crushes her to death.
* **The Cover-Up:** To conceal the murder, William fakes a gas leak, causing the grand opening of Circus Baby's Pizza World to be canceled. The animatronics are moved underground to an engineering facility: **Circus Baby's Entertainment & Rental**. Elizabeth's soul possesses Circus Baby.

### 2. The Scooping Incident (Sister Location)
Years later, a grown **Michael Afton** is sent by his father, William, down into the subterranean facility to "find his sister" and "put her back together." 
* **The Trap:** The Funtime Animatronics, desperate to escape their underground prison where they are continuously shocked and disassembled, plot against Michael. Led by Circus Baby (who is now heavily influenced by Elizabeth's consciousness), they tear themselves apart and combine their mechanical endoskeletons into a singular, amorphous metallic entity known as **Ennard**.
* **The Scooper:** Ennard lures Michael into the Scooping Room. The Scooper (a device designed to inject and extract Remnant) eviscerates Michael's internal organs, and Ennard climbs inside Michael's hollowed-out skin to use him as a human disguise to walk free in the outside world.
* **The Reanimation:** Over days, Michael's body begins to decay and turn purple. Ennard is forced to abandon the rotting flesh, escaping into the city sewers. However, due to the high concentration of Remnant injected into Michael's body by the Scooper, his soul remains bound to his decaying flesh. Michael stands up, miraculously surviving his own physical death. He vows to hunt down his father to end the cycle of terror.

## ERA 4: The Grand Re-Opening & The Bite of '87 (1987)

### 1. The Toy Animatronics (FNAF 2)
In 1987, Fazbear Entertainment attempts a massive corporate rebrand. They open a brand-new, high-budget **Freddy Fazbear's Pizza**. 
* **The New Roster:** The original 1985 animatronics are left in the Parts & Service room to be used for spare parts. They are replaced by the high-tech **Toy Animatronics** (Toy Freddy, Toy Bonnie, Toy Chica, Mangle, Balloon Boy).
* **The Security Systems:** To prevent another tragedy, the Toy animatronics are equipped with facial recognition software tied directly to a local criminal database. 

### 2. The Second Missing Children's Incident (DCI)
William Afton infiltrates the new location using a fake alias (securing the night shift position before transferring to the day shift). 
* **The Act:** Using the Golden Freddy suit, William lures and murders a *second* group of five children within the pizzerias corridors (as depicted in the *SAVETHEM* death minigames).
* **The Corruption:** The dynamic souls of these new victims corrupt the programming of the Toy animatronics. The Puppet is also present at this location, trying to help. Because of their corrupted software and supernatural influence, the Toy animatronics become increasingly hostile toward adults and police staff.

### 3. The Bite of '87
As the situation spirals out of control, a daytime birthday party is scheduled before the location is forced to close down. 
* **The Attack:** Security guard **Jeremy Fitzgerald** (who survived five nights on the night shift) is assigned to day-shift protection duties near the stage. 
* **The Culprit:** **Mangle** (or potentially another Toy animatronic), malfunctioned by its corrupted recognition system or supernatural anger, lunges forward and bites off Jeremy's frontal lobe.
* **The Fallout:** Jeremy survives, but the public relations nightmare forces Fazbear Entertainment to scrap the expensive Toy animatronics completely. The location is shut down after only a few weeks of operation. The company promises to eventually reopen on a much lower budget, utilizing the original, retrofitted 1985 models.

## ERA 5: The Budget Era & The Demise of William Afton (1992/1993)

### 1. The FNAF 1 Location
In the early 1990s, a cash-strapped Fazbear Entertainment reopens a downscaled version of the pizzeria utilizing the original building from 1985. The original characters (Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, Foxy) are repaired and put back on stage.

* **Michael's Investigation:** Operating under the pseudonym **Mike Schmidt**, Michael Afton takes the night guard job. He uses this opportunity to investigate the pizzerias and find clues about his father's whereabouts. He survives the week despite the animatronics mistaking him for his father, William, but is eventually fired for "tampering with the animatronics" and "odor" (due to his decaying physical state).
* **The Fate of Phone Guy:** During this era, the long-term head of security, **Phone Guy**, is cornered and killed by the possessed animatronics during his final shift.

### 2. The Springlock Trap (The "Follow Me" Minigames)
After the FNAF 1 location closes its doors for good due to bankruptcy, William Afton returns to the abandoned, rotting building to harvest the possessed metal from his original victims.
* **The Destruction of the Classics:** Over several nights, William ambushes Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, and Foxy one by one, dismantling them with axes/tools to collect their infected endoskeletons.
* **The Retribution:** By destroying the physical hulls, William inadvertently releases the spirits of the five children. The ghosts corner William inside the hidden, leaking Safe Room.
* **The Springlock Failure:** Terrified and desperate, William spots the old **Spring Bonnie** suit in the corner. He slides inside it, laughing at the ghosts, thinking he is safe. However, rain dripping from the decaying roof leaks directly onto him. The moisture triggers the old springlocks. 
* **The Birth of Springtrap:** The mechanical parts snap shut, piercing William's flesh and shattering his bones. He undergoes a agonizing, protracted death sequence. The spirits, vindicated, fade away. The Safe Room is sealed shut by building inspectors with William's trapped, corpse-fused body left inside to rot for thirty years.

## ERA 6: The Modern Horror Attraction & The Flame (2023)

### 1. Fazbear's Fright (FNAF 3)
Thirty years after the closure of the final pizzeria, an independent group capitalized on urban legends and old ghost stories by building **Fazbear's Fright: The Horror Attraction** inside an amusement park.
* **The Discovery:** While searching the ruins of the old pizzeria, the attraction creators break through the false wall of the Safe Room and discover the mummified, springlocked corpse of William Afton, which has become supernaturally animated. He is now **Springtrap**.
* **The Night Shift:** Michael Afton (or another anonymous protagonist) takes the job at Fazbear's Fright. Springtrap stalks the guard, fueled by undying malice.
* **The Fire:** At the end of the week, the attraction is burned to the ground due to "faulty wiring." This is highly implied to be a deliberate act by the protagonist to incinerate Springtrap. However, Springtrap survives the fire and escapes into the night, eventually rebuilding his damaged frame using spare costume parts (becoming **Scraptrap**).

## ERA 7: The Final Purge (The FNAF 6 Era)

### 1. Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria Simulator (FNAF 6)
Henry Emily returns from hiding after discovering that the souls of the children—including his daughter Charlie—and his former partner William are still roaming the earth. He designs a massive, final trap.
* **The Labyrinth:** Henry creates a fake Fazbear franchise location. Underneath the building is an inescapable, structural labyrinth equipped with high-intensity thermal heaters. He hires a manager—who turns out to be **Michael Afton**—to manage the storefront and salvage rogue animatronics from the back alleyways.
* **The Gathering:** Henry successfully lures the remaining possessed entities to the site:
  1. **Scraptrap** (William Afton)
  2. **Scrap Baby** (Elizabeth Afton, who was kicked out of Ennard)
  3. **Molten Freddy** (The remains of Ennard, consisting of the 1985 MCI remnant)
  4. **Lefty** (An advanced containment capsule Henry built to capture and hold the **Puppet/Charlie**)

> [!danger] The Ultimate Fire
> Once all entities are contained inside the subterranean maze, Henry overrides the facility controls and seals all exits. He sets the facility ablaze. 
> * Michael Afton chooses to stay and die in the fire, finally letting his soul rest. 
> * Henry dies alongside his creations.
> * Elizabeth, Charlie, and the original 1985 souls are finally released from their physical prisons, ascending to peace.
> * Henry condemns William Afton to the deepest pits of hell.

### 2. Ultimate Custom Night (UCN)
Instead of crossing over or allowing William Afton to pass into hell, one specific vengeful soul—**Cassidy / Andrew** ("The One You Shouldn't Have Killed")—holds William back.
* **The Purgatory:** The vengeful spirit constructs an eternal, shifting nightmare realm inside William's subconscious. William is subjected to an endless loop of being hunted and brutally slaughtered by manifestations of every animatronic from his past. He is kept alive supernaturally purely to ensure his torment never ends.

## ERA 8: The Digital Age & The Rise of the Mimic

### 1. Five Nights at Freddy's: Help Wanted (The VR Game)
In an effort to clean up its reputation after the devastating fires and conspiracy lawsuits, a corporate revival of Fazbear Entertainment hires an indie game developer to make games mocking the rumors. They then commission a state-of-the-art Virtual Reality game: *The Freddy Fazbear Virtual Experience*.
* **The Circuit Boards:** To save time on programming complex AI pathfinding, the developers scan old, salvaged animatronic circuit boards sent to them by Fazbear Entertainment. One of these boards belongs to an ancient endoskeleton designed to mimic human behavior (The **Mimic**).
* **The Birth of Glitchtrap:** The digital scan accidentally uploads the Mimic's programming—which had observed and internalized William Afton's horrific acts—into the game's code. This manifests as a digital entity shaped like a stitched bunny costume: **Glitchtrap**.
* **The Corruption of Vanessa:** A beta tester named **Vanessa** is targeted by Glitchtrap. The virus manipulates her mind, fracturing her consciousness and creating a secondary, murderous persona within her: **Vanny**.

### 2. Security Breach & Ruin
Fazbear Entertainment opens its crowning achievement: the **Freddy Fazbear Mega Pizzaplex**, a massive multi-level mall powered by advanced, self-aware Glamrock animatronics.
* **The Control:** Operating under Glitchtrap's influence, Vanessa works as the head security guard at the Pizzaplex. Deep beneath the Pizzaplex, in the excavated ruins of the FNAF 6 burning site, the physical Mimic endoskeleton is trapped. Vanny uses the Pizzaplex resources to construct a flesh-and-metal fusion body for it (manifesting as **Burntrap**), or uses its digital influence to spread a virus throughout the entire Pizzaplex network.
* **Gregory and Freddy:** A homeless youth named **Gregory** gets trapped overnight. He teams up with **Glamrock Freddy** (who experienced a system crash that disconnected him from the Pizzaplex network). Together, they dismantle the corrupted Glamrock animatronics (Chica, Roxy, Monty) and escape, eventually triggering the destruction or systematic abandonment of the Mega Pizzaplex (leading into the events of *Ruin* where Cassie explores the decaying remains to save Gregory, encountering the raw, unbound **Mimic** endoskeleton).

## ERA 9: Into the Pit (The Echoes of Agony)

### 1. The Modern Context of Oswald
Decades after the closure of the original 1985 pizzerias and following the eventual decline of the broader Fazbear franchise, a young boy named **Oswald** lives in a economically depressed, decaying town. His father struggles to find stable work, and Oswald spends his boring summer days exploring the town.

### 2. The Mechanics of the Ballpit
Oswald discovers an old, dirty ballpit inside **Jeff's Pizza**—which is structurally built directly on top of the original boarded-up 1985 Freddy Fazbear's Pizza location.
* **The Agony Matrix:** The ballpit acts as a physical conduit for **Agony**. The pure emotional horror generated by William Afton's slaughter of the five children on June 26, 1985, became physically embedded into the environment, creating a pocket dimension or a chronological echo.

### 3. The Encounter with the Yellow Rabbit
When Oswald submerges himself in the ballpit, he is pulled back through time into a vivid, hyper-real loop of the **1985 tragedy**. 
* **The Entity:** The primary antagonist of *Into the Pit* is not the literal human William Afton, but rather a demonic, organic-mechanical amalgamation known as the **Yellow Rabbit (Pit Bonnie)**. This creature is a monster birthed purely from the concentrated **Agony** left behind by Afton's original crimes.
* **The Switch:** The Yellow Rabbit escapes the ballpit into the modern day, dragging Oswald's father into the pit and taking his place at home. The creature mimics human behavior, though it appears as a terrifying plush monster to Oswald, while the rest of the world sees it as a normal human father due to a perception-altering psychic effect caused by its supernatural origin.
* **The Resolution:** Oswald fights back, returns to the ballpit dimension, and manages to hang the Yellow Rabbit entity using old ropes and nets within the pit structure. The monster suffocates/dissolves into dust, and Oswald frees his real father, sealing or abandoning the ballpit. 

> [!keypoint] Summary Conclusion
> *Into the Pit* serves as a profound thematic exploration of the FNAF timeline: it proves that even if physical animatronics are burned, destroyed, or salvaged, the **Agony** of William Afton’s initial 1985 murders remains permanently anchored to the earth, capable of manifesting new nightmares independent of Afton's living soul.
